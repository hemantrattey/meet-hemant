Who I am

The kind of person who actually shows up

I'm curious about pretty much everything, and I tend to get genuinely invested in the people I care about. I'll remember the random story you told me three weeks ago, send you something because it made me think of you, and probably ask way too many questions about whatever you're currently obsessed with. I like making plans, but I'm equally happy with a spontaneous "there are tickets tonight" decision. I'm ambitious and pretty driven, but I don't want my life to be all work. I care a lot about my friends, love hosting people, cook like I'm feeding twice as many people as are actually coming, and will happily turn an ordinary Tuesday into dinner, drinks, a game, or a last-minute concert. I'm a little intense about the things I love — music, sports, food, photography — but I think enthusiasm is a good quality to have. Mostly, I'm looking for something that feels like having a best friend you also really want to kiss. Someone to build a life with, laugh at stupid things with, and keep choosing each other when life gets busy.

Mission statement

What I'm actually looking for

Someone to build a life with — preferably one involving good food, ridiculous concerts, spontaneous plans, and a lot of laughing at things that aren't actually that funny.

Plot twist

I can name every player on six different teams

Cricket, Basketball, Soccer, F1 — I watch all of it and I have opinions. Strong ones. This is a feature, not a bug.

Value proposition

I notice the little things.

I remember what you said you were stressed about last week. I'll send you a song because I think you'll like it. I'll ask follow-up questions about the thing you're excited about even if I know absolutely nothing about it. And if you've had a terrible day, there's probably food involved.

Non-negotiable

The cats were here first. We are a package deal.

Their names are Olly and Ozzy. They have opinions about guests. They will judge you slowly. They will eventually sit on you — which is basically a formal endorsement.

Current chapter

Building AI & machine learning products for a living

Most of my time goes into AI workflows, ML applications, and data-driven products — somewhere between engineering, product thinking, and making AI genuinely useful for real people. Photography used to be the full-time world; now it's a creative outlet without the 3am client edits.

Full disclosure

Green flags and honest warnings — everything you'd normally find out by month two, disclosed up front.

GREEN FLAGI remember the little things

Tell me something once and there's a decent chance I'll remember it three weeks later. If you're important to me, I'm going to pay attention.

GREEN FLAGYour wins become my wins

I genuinely like seeing people I care about succeed. I will celebrate your promotion, your weird side project, your 5K, your good presentation, whatever it is. Probably louder than necessary.

GREEN FLAGI show up

If you're having a rough week, I won't necessarily know how to fix it. But I'll check in, bring food, listen to you complain, and make sure you know you're not dealing with it alone.

GREEN FLAGI will make ordinary days feel like something

Doesn't always need to be a big date. Sometimes it's cooking dinner, watching a game, finding a new bar, or deciding at 7pm that we should go somewhere.

RED FLAGI can be a little intense

When I love something, I really love it. Music, food, sports, work, people. “Casually interested” isn't really my setting.

RED FLAGI talk to animals

Full conversations. Different voices. Zero shame. They have opinions and unfortunately I respect them.
